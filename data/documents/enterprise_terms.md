# Enterprise Terms of Service

## Contract Structure
Enterprise customers operate under either the Standard Enterprise Agreement
or the Annual Master Agreement (AMA). The AMA permits negotiated custom terms.

## Refund-Related Clauses
Under the Standard Enterprise Agreement, refunds follow the general Refund
Policy (30-day window). Under the AMA, refund terms are governed by Schedule C
if one is attached; otherwise the standard 30-day window applies.

## Support Tiers
Enterprise customers receive Priority or Premium support depending on tier.
