# Customer Support Policy

## Support Channels
Enterprise customers can reach support via the priority ticket queue, email,
or the dedicated Slack Connect channel for Premium tier accounts.

## Response Times
Priority tier: first response within 4 business hours.
Premium tier: first response within 1 business hour, 24/7 for critical severity.

## Escalation
Unresolved tickets older than 48 hours automatically escalate to a senior
support engineer.
